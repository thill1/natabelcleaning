How does the Instant Quote work for homes?

Residential Instant Quote is live. In about 90 seconds you enter ZIP, home size, and frequency, then see the cleaning price before sharing your name and contact details.

Can I price move-in or move-out online?

Yes. Move-In / Move-Out pricing is included in the home quote flow using your home-size tier plus NataBel’s approved move-service premium.

What does the Instant Quote price include?

The amount shown is for cleaning service only. Optional add-ons are quoted separately and confirmed by Fatima.

Why does commercial cleaning use a walkthrough?

Facility use, access, frequency, floor types, and scope vary too much for a responsible one-size-fits-all number. The online request prepares a walkthrough and follow-up quote.

Where does NataBel clean?

NataBel is based in Rocklin and serves nearby Placer County communities first, with selected Sacramento-side ZIP coverage based on route and scheduling.

What is the 24-hour Pristine Guarantee?

If an area included in the agreed scope was missed, report it within 24 hours and NataBel will return to re-clean that area at no additional charge.

Do you show ratings and review counts?

Not until real customer reviews exist. NataBel does not publish invented ratings or review totals.

The NataBel standard

The Pristine Checklist.

Fatima inspects against this written list before a clean is called done. Items noted “upon request” are available as add-ons.

Kitchen 11 points
  • Appliance exteriors cleaned (interiors upon request)
  • Microwave exterior and interior cleaned
  • Tables and chairs cleaned
  • Countertops and backsplash disinfected (all items removed and replaced)
  • Cabinet fronts cleaned
  • Sinks scrubbed and disinfected (dishes upon request)
  • Floor vacuumed and/or washed
  • Windowsills cleaned (blinds dusted upon request)
  • Baseboards dusted
  • Fingerprints removed from woodwork, doorframes, and switch plates
  • Trash emptied
Bedrooms 11 points
  • Beds made (linens changed upon request)
  • Lamps cleaned and lampshades dusted
  • Picture frames dusted
  • Furniture dusted on top, front, and underneath
  • Floors vacuumed and/or washed (under beds when accessible)
  • Windowsills cleaned (blinds dusted upon request)
  • Baseboards dusted
  • Fingerprints removed from woodwork, doorframes, and switch plates
  • Trash emptied
  • Mirrors cleaned
  • General straightening completed
Bathrooms 12 points
  • Tub and shower tiles scrubbed, disinfected, and rinsed
  • Toilets disinfected inside and out
  • Chrome fixtures cleaned and shined
  • Towels neatly hung and folded
  • Countertops disinfected (all items removed and replaced)
  • Cabinet fronts cleaned
  • Sinks scrubbed and disinfected
  • Floors vacuumed and/or washed
  • Windowsills cleaned (blinds dusted upon request)
  • Baseboards dusted
  • Trash emptied
  • Mirrors cleaned
Other living areas 15 points
  • Upholstered furniture vacuumed
  • Cushions and pillows fluffed and straightened
  • Ceiling fans dusted (height restrictions apply)
  • Lamps cleaned and lampshades dusted
  • Picture frames dusted
  • Furniture dusted on top, front, and underneath
  • Hardwood floors vacuumed and/or washed
  • Tile floors vacuumed and/or washed
  • Hallways and stairs vacuumed and/or washed
  • Carpets vacuumed
  • Windowsills cleaned (blinds dusted upon request)
  • Baseboards dusted
  • Fingerprints removed from woodwork, doorframes, and switch plates
  • Trash emptied
  • General straightening completed
Final quality check 7 checks
  • All requested rooms completed
  • Special client instructions reviewed and completed
  • Add-on services completed, if applicable
  • Rooms marked Do Not Enter / Do Not Clean were respected
  • Personal items returned to their original locations
  • Cleaning supplies and equipment removed
  • Home given a final walkthrough

Scope is confirmed before the first visit. Anything outside this list is quoted separately so there are no surprises.
