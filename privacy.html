This policy explains how NataBel Pristine Cleaning collects, uses, and protects personal information from quote requests, contact forms, and job applications.

Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to visitors and customers who submit information on natabelpristinecleaning.com, including Instant Quote requests, contact forms, and employment applications.

Information we collect

Quote and contact requests: name, phone, email, city, ZIP, service address, service details, and optional notes.
Job applications: name, phone, email, address, availability, work history, references, and electronic signature details you provide.
Technical data: landing page, referrer, UTM parameters, and basic browser request metadata needed to operate the site.

How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to requests, provide quotes, schedule service, review applicants, communicate about open roles, and improve operations. We do not publish customer personal data and we do not sell personal information.

How data is shared

Form submissions are delivered to NataBel via secure service providers used for email and hosting infrastructure. Information is shared only with vendors needed to process requests or where required by law.

California privacy rights

California residents may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable exceptions. You may also request details about categories of information collected and disclosed for business purposes.

Data retention

We keep business records for as long as reasonably needed for customer support, hiring decisions, legal compliance, and operational documentation.

Children’s privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
