Meet Fatima.
The heart behind NataBel.

NataBel Pristine Cleaning was founded by Fatima Patalano — a Rocklin local on a mission to build the cleaning company she always wished existed: meticulous, reliable, genuinely kind, and rooted in this community.

The Story

Cleaning, done the way
it should be done.

I started NataBel because I kept hearing the same story from homeowners and business owners across Rocklin and Placer County: cleaners who didn't show up, who cut corners, who treated the work as an afterthought. I knew it could be different — because I knew how I'd want my home and my business cleaned.

So I built NataBel around a simple standard: hotel-grade cleanliness, delivered by people you actually trust. Every team member is background-checked and trained to the same meticulous checklist. Every recurring client gets the same crew, every visit. Every commercial account gets a custom scope of work and a single point of contact.

We're based right here in Rocklin. We clean in the neighborhoods we live in. When you call NataBel, you're talking to the owner — not a call center. That matters to me, and I think it matters to you too.

— Fatima Patalano, Founder
